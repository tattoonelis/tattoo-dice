ABOUT

Tattoo Dice is a creative tattoo idea generator for tattoo artists, people looking for their next tattoo and anyone who loves to draw.

Roll one, two or three dice to discover unexpected combinations of carefully hand-picked subjects. Choose your Main subject or switch to Random and leave the entire tattoo concept to chance.

Looking for fresh tattoo inspiration? Stuck on what to draw? Or just curious to see which random tattoo idea the dice throw at you? Tattoo Dice offers creative drawing prompts to help get your imagination moving again.

Tattoo Dice is not an AI image generator. It brings hand-selected tattoo subjects together to inspire an original idea. What you create with that idea is entirely up to you.

Whether you’re designing a tattoo, searching for your next piece or simply sketching for fun, we hope Tattoo Dice gives you a fun place to start.

Happy rolling!
